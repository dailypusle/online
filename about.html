About us

News that keeps you in the know

DailyPulse puts the day's headlines from trusted publishers in one fast, clean place, so you spend less time hunting for the news and more time understanding it.

Why we started

Good news is scattered. We pull it together.

Staying informed usually means opening a dozen sites, closing a dozen pop-ups, and scrolling past things you never asked for.

DailyPulse started with a simple question: what if the day's important stories, from Nigeria and around the world, were waiting in one place, with a clear photo, a clear headline, and a short summary?

That's the whole idea. Every story links straight back to the publisher that reported it.

What's in it for you

Your time, back

What we stand for

Principles we actually follow

  • Credit the source. Every headline and photo links to the original publisher. We point you to the story, we don't copy it.
  • Show real pictures. Stories without a clear, sharp image are skipped, so the page stays clean.
  • Respect your privacy. The "Near me" button uses your approximate location only to find your country. DailyPulse does not store it.
  • Separate fact from opinion. Our assistant is told to explain confirmed facts apart from interpretation, and not to invent details.
